# WakeProof Legal

Public legal documentation for **WakeProof**, a mobile alarm app built around scan missions,
wake checks, and local-first history.

## Documents

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) | Data processing, permissions, retention, deletion, and third-party services |
| [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) | Service limitations, acceptable use, purchases, and platform terms |

## Product principles

- Alarm settings, scan-mission hashes, and wake-up history remain on the device.
- Camera frames are processed locally and are not stored.
- The app does not require a WakeProof account and does not contain advertising.
- Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process purchase data required for entitlement management.
- The legal documents cover both iOS and Android behavior.
